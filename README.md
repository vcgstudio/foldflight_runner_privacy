# FoldFlight Runner: Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** November 27, 2026
**Developer:** VCG Studio ("we", "us")
**App:** FoldFlight Runner, for Android, iOS (where released) and the web (CrazyGames)

FoldFlight Runner is a paper-plane endless runner. This policy explains what information the game handles, why, and the choices you have.

## Summary

- You do not need an account to play. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location.
- Your game progress and settings are stored **on your device** (on the web, in your browser or through CrazyGames). We do not run our own servers and do not receive this data.
- The game contains no analytics or crash-reporting services of our own, and no in-app purchases.
- The game shows **rewarded ads only when you choose to watch one**. On mobile they come from Google AdMob; on the web, from CrazyGames. These providers may collect information as described below.
- We do not sell personal information.

## 1. Information stored on your device

The game saves the following so that you can continue where you left off:

- coin balance, best distance and best score;
- planes, skins and booster upgrades you own or have equipped;
- daily challenge progress;
- settings: music and sound volume and reduced motion, tutorial progress, and whether you have seen this privacy policy;
- run identifiers generated on the device, used only to prevent a reward from being granted twice.

On Android and iOS this data stays in the app's local storage on your device. It is not sent to us.

On the web, when you play on CrazyGames, it is saved through CrazyGames' game-data feature. That data is kept in your browser, or with your CrazyGames account if you are signed in, under the CrazyGames privacy policy.

**Deleting it:** uninstall the app or clear its storage (mobile), or clear your browser's site data or your CrazyGames data (web). We cannot delete it for you, because we never receive it.

## 2. Advertising

### Mobile (Android and iOS): Google AdMob

Ads appear only when you tap a button offering a reward, such as a revive or a coin bonus. To serve, limit, measure and personalize ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process information such as:

- your device's advertising identifier;
- your IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
- device and app information;
- how you interact with ads.

Google acts under its own policies:

- How Google uses information from apps that use its services: <https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites>
- Google Privacy Policy: <https://policies.google.com/privacy>

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the app shows Google's consent message before personalized ads are used. You can change your choice from the app's settings, where available.

The privacy policy screen shown when you first open the game is for information only: closing it with Continue is not consent to personalized advertising.

You can also control personalized advertising at any time in your device settings:

- **Android:** Settings › Google › Ads (reset or delete your advertising ID).
- **iOS:** Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking, and Apple Advertising.

### Web: CrazyGames

The web version is published on CrazyGames, which serves ads and operates its own services (including the game-data feature above) under its own privacy policy: <https://www.crazygames.com/privacy-policy>. Ads appear in the game only when you choose a rewarded option.

## 3. Sharing your results

After a run you can tap **Share**. The game creates a result image and text (distance, score, coins and your paper plane, without names or identifiers) and hands them to your device's share sheet or your browser. You choose the app or contact; the game does not post anything by itself and does not receive what happens next. If sharing is not available, the text is copied to your clipboard instead.

## 4. Information we do not collect

We do not collect or store, on any server of ours:

- personal identifiers;
- precise location;
- contacts, photos or files;
- microphone or camera data;
- health or financial data.

The game does not use Unity Analytics, Unity Cloud Diagnostics, Firebase or Google Analytics. On Android, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the game includes Google's own measurement libraries, which Google may use when serving and measuring ads (see section 2). The game shows no permission prompts and never asks for camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos or files. On Android it declares: internet and network-state access; the advertising ID and Android's ad-services permissions, used by Google Mobile Ads; and wake-lock and foreground-service permissions, used by Google's libraries to schedule background tasks.

## 5. Children

FoldFlight Runner is a general-audience game. It is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help, including by contacting the relevant ad provider.

## 6. Sharing and sale

We do not share or sell personal information. The only third parties that may receive information from the game are the advertising and platform providers named above, which act under their own policies, and the app you choose when you share a result.

## 7. Security and retention

Game data lives on your device or with CrazyGames and is kept until you delete it (see section 1). Ad providers keep data according to their own retention policies.

## 8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA/CPRA), you may have rights to:

- access, correct or delete personal information;
- object to, or withdraw consent for, personalized advertising.

Because we hold no personal information about you, these requests are generally handled by the providers above or through your device settings. If you need help, contact us.

## 9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game changes, for example if a new feature or service provider is added. The new version will be published at this address with a new effective date, and the game will show it to you again.

## 10. Contact

Questions or requests about privacy: open an issue at <https://github.com/vcgstudio/foldflight_runner_privacy/issues>.
